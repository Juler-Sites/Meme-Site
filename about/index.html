About

I'm Julian Robbins — a one-person shop in Little Rock. I do hands-on break-fix work, and what I actually enjoy is figuring out why something broke.

That includes Windows troubleshooting, hardware swaps, data recovery from failing drives, and bringing dead or obsolete systems back up: AT&T U-verse and Microsoft Mediaroom gear, Dish receivers, Comcast RDK boxes, MIPS firmware, and old operating systems running on modern hardware.

Win10MenuBridge

Remote PC Help

My remote support service for Windows machines — troubleshooting, printers, Wi-Fi, cleanup, data recovery, and the like. Details, prices, and how to reach me are on the Remote PC Help page.
